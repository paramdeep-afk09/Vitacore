About VitaCore

More than a club.

VitaCore is the departmental club of the Faculty of Allied Health Sciences at CGC University, founded in 2025. More than an event platform, VitaCore creates opportunities for students to step beyond the classroom through educational trips, workshops, campus activities, competitions and collaborative experiences — turning curiosity into participation and everyday university life into something worth remembering.
